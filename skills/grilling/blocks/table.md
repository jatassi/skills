# `table` tables

Exactly one GFM table, drawn with the page's own styles.

## Fit and readability

- Put the thing being compared in the first column (one option or candidate per row) and one attribute per column.
- Put the attributes that decide the question first, and keep cells to a few words. The pane can be as narrow as 480 px.
- Bold the recommended cell or cells (`**40 ms**`).

## Anchors

A comment names a cell by its row's first-column text and its column header, such as `cell row "MCP server", column "Install"`. Keep first-column entries and headers short and unique. A repeated label gets its position appended, and an empty one is named by position alone.

## Marks and theme

Bold is the table's only mark, standing in for `recommended`. Write `risk` or `muted` in words. Tables always follow the theme and take no override.
