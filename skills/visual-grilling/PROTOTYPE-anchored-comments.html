Anchored comments: what does the agent get when you click a spot?

The question. On a round page you can click any spot in a question's illustration and leave a comment on it. The agent that drew the illustration later reads that comment without seeing the page. What description of the spot lets it tell what you meant: the diagram's own ids, the element's text, its role or label, a CSS selector, click coordinates, or a small picture of the area?

Turn on Comment mode, hover an illustration to see what a click would point at, click to comment. The right-hand panel shows exactly what the agent would read, and lets you switch each kind of detail on or off. Each card has a "What the agent wrote" section so you can check whether the description maps back to it.
